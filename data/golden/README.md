# Golden set v0 — broadcast (Spike SP-01 / M1-CV-03)

20 broadcast matches, 15-minute in-play segment each, taken from official league / federation / club / rights-holder YouTube channels.

| Group | Count | Matches |
|---|---|---|
| Men | 8 | MLS, U.S. Open Cup, Concacaf CC, J1 League, A-League, AFC CL Elite, AFC CL Two, Scottish Premiership |
| Women | 7 | WSL 2, UEFA Women's Nations League, NWSL, UWCL final, AFC Women's CL, UWCL qualifying, NCAA |
| Youth | 5 | UEFA Youth League U19, MLS NEXT U16, FIFA U-17 WC 2005, J.League U-18, U-17 Spain v Germany |

Frame rates: 50 fps (SPFL), 60 fps (4), 25 fps (3), 30 fps (rest). All 1080p sources (the 2005 FIFA final is an SD 4:3 upscale with side bars).

## Hard cases worth knowing

- **Strong sun/shadow split**: WSL 2 Bristol v Southampton, Spain v Germany U-17
- **Night / floodlights**: NWSL final, Juventus v Hammarby, FC Seoul v Melbourne
- **Single-camera youth production on a training complex**: MLS NEXT U16
- **SD 4:3 with decorative side bars**: FIFA U-17 2005
- **Channel watermark over the pitch**: SPFL "CLASSIC MATCH", FIFA TV
- **Frequent replays / close-ups**: Concacaf, U.S. Open Cup, AFC CL Two final

## Files

- `segments.txt` — `id|category|match|channel|segment start (mm:ss)`; segment = start + 15 min
- `raw/<id>.mp4` — downloaded segment (not committed)
- `sheets/<id>.jpg` — contact sheet of minutes 0–46 of the source video (6×4, 2-min steps), used to pick the in-play segment
- `manifest.json` / `manifest.csv` — generated by `python3 build_manifest.py` with actual fps, resolution, duration, size
- `dl.sh` — downloader (`yt-dlp --download-sections`), skips files already present

## Usage restrictions

Internal R&D and evaluation only. Do not redistribute, publish, or use frames in marketing. These videos are copyrighted by their leagues/broadcasters; YouTube download falls under the G-LEGAL review in PLAN v1.1. Delete `raw/` if legal review requires it.
